How this works

PRAMANA proposes relationships, then executes statistical tests on the data. It corrects for testing multiple claims together and checks the configured effect floor and direction. Only claims that PASS become findings and enter memory; PASS is evidence for this dataset, not a causal guarantee.

Limits to keep in mind
  • No confounder adjustment or held-out replication: a real association need not be causal.
  • Effect floors do not separate truth from chance; corrected significance matters too. Small real effects can pass.
  • Categorical-versus-categorical claims and confidence intervals are not supported in V1.
  • Tables with a usable trend axis are limited to 3,000 rows by the quadratic trend estimator.
  • One run at a time; up to eight proposed claims per V1 run. Verification can take several minutes.
  • Effect strength labels use configured metric bands, not universal clinical or practical importance.
  • The optional LLM generator has only a small live validation; its refusal rate at scale is unmeasured.
